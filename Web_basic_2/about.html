01 / OUR STORYWHY WE EXIST

A BIG
DECISION.
A MISSING
GUIDE.

Having the money to buy a car and knowing which car to buy are two very different things.

In India, a car often represents years of hard work, a personal milestone and a source of pride. But the moment you begin looking, the choices can feel endless. Brands compete for attention, models come in multiple variants, engines and gearboxes have their own complexities, and every showroom has a pitch. It can be difficult to separate what you genuinely need from what you are being encouraged to buy.

We believe too many buyers are left to figure it all out on their own. Important questions about reliability, maintenance, real ownership costs and long-term suitability may go unanswered. The fear of being misled, paying for things you don't need or choosing a car that doesn't fit your life is real. Gaadi Sarathi was created to make that journey clearer — by bringing the information that matters to the buyer, before the purchase is made.
